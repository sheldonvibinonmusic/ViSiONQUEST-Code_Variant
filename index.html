When context fades, restarting costs more.

The code may still be there. What fades is the reason behind it: the original goal, the choices you made, and the next step.

Three things go missing

Lost intent

The files remain, but the original problem, promise, and MVP scope fade.

Lost reasoning

Commits show what changed—not why an approach failed, what you learned, or which blocker remains.

Stranded value

Useful components, schemas, research, and lessons stay buried instead of becoming a starting point for the next idea.

The work is not gone. Its story is scattered.
